# Links, backlinks & graph

## Wikilinks

Type `[[` and start typing a page name to link to it. Links look like `[[Page name]]` in the file, or `[[Page name|custom text]]` with an alias. Click a link to open the page; clicking a link to a page that doesn't exist yet creates it.

Regular markdown links (`[text](Other%20page.md)`) and web links work too.

## Backlinks

At the bottom of every page:

- **Linked mentions** — every page that links here, with the sentence the link sits in.
- **Unlinked mentions** — pages that mention this page's name without linking to it.

## Graph view

Open **Graph view** from the sidebar or press `Ctrl G`. Each dot is a page and each line a link.

- Colors group pages by top-level folder.
- Hover a page to highlight its neighbours; click to open it.
- Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, drag a dot to move it.
- Filter by name, and hide orphan pages or links to pages that don't exist yet.

From a page's `•••` menu, **Open local graph** shows only the pages around it.

## Page outline

Pages with three or more headings show a small outline on the right edge. Hover it to see the headings and click one to jump there.
